How we handle your information

Privacy notice

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it, written plainly, because a notice nobody reads protects nobody.

Last updated 20 September 2026

The short version. A parent or guardian holds the account. We collect what is needed to teach your child and to show you how they are getting on, and nothing for advertising. We do not sell data, we do not track you across other websites, and we never see your card details.

Who we are

Y7to11.AI is operated by IntelliQuest Solutions Limited, a company registered in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at privacy@y7to11.ai.

What we collect

You, as the account holder

The contract and the payment are with you, so we keep a record of who you are:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address: this is how we reach you, and it is the identity the account is built on
  • Your mobile number: so we can contact you about the account or a safeguarding concern without waiting on email
  • Your relationship to the child, and your chosen username
  • Your password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash, which we cannot read. Nobody at IntelliQuest can tell you what it is

We use your name, email address and mobile number to run your account and to reach you about it. We do not use them for marketing unless you ask us to, we do not pass them to anyone for their own marketing, and we never sell them.

The child who will be learning

Two things, and nothing else. About your child we hold their first name and their date of birth. No surname, no school, no address, no photograph, no contact details of their own.

The first name is so the app can address them, and so that a safeguarding alert can tell you which child it concerns. The date of birth is used to work out one thing: which school year they are in, which decides the curriculum they are taught.

We do not ask for the school year separately, because the date of birth already answers it. In England the year group is fixed by a child's age on the 31st of August, so asking for both would be collecting a second piece of personal information that tells us nothing the first one does not.

We also store the exam board and tier chosen for each subject. Those describe the course, not the child, and cannot identify anybody.

Why so little, deliberately

The ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code asks services likely to be used by children to collect the minimum personal data needed for each element of the service, and to turn off anything that is not needed. We have taken that literally:

  • A surname was never needed for anything a first name does not do, so it is not asked for
  • The school name was never used at all, so it is not asked for
  • The school year is worked out rather than collected
  • Nothing about your child is used for profiling, advertising, or any decision other than which lesson to show them

Their learning

  • Which topics were opened, and in which mode
  • Answers given, whether they were right, and the marks awarded
  • How long questions took, and the mastery score calculated from that work
  • Messages sent to the tutor, and its replies

Progress is recorded from marked work only. We do not infer anything from how long a page was left open, and we do not ask a child to rate themselves and store that as fact.

Payment

Card payments are processed entirely by Stripe. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's own secure page and never pass through our servers, so we neither see nor store them. We keep only your subscription status, the plan, and Stripe's customer and subscription references.

Why we hold it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service you bought: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the account secure: our legitimate interest in preventing unauthorised access, including rate limiting and sign-in records.
  • To protect children: our legitimate interest, and our safeguarding responsibilities, in reviewing messages that raise a concern.
  • To take payment: performance of our contract, and our legal obligation to keep financial records.

Children's information

This service is bought and set up by an adult, and the account belongs to that adult. A child uses it under the account you control. We ask for a child's details only where they change what is taught: their school year decides the curriculum, and their exam board and tier decide the questions.

You can see everything held about your child, which is a first name and a date of birth. You can correct it, or delete the account and all of it, from Account and privacy inside the app.

The AI tutor

When your child asks the tutor a question, the question and the topic they are working on are sent to a language model running in Microsoft Azure, within our own tenant. It is not used to train anyone's model.

Your child's profile details are deliberately never included in what is sent. Their name, date of birth, school and contact details are not part of any prompt.

Lessons, worked examples and questions are not generated while your child waits. They were written in advance and are served from our own store, so most of what they read involves no model call at all.

Safeguarding

Every message a child writes to the tutor is checked before it is sent anywhere. If one raises a safeguarding concern, we do three things: answer the child by pointing them to a trusted adult, record the message, and email the adult on the account.

That alert names the child and the rule that was triggered. It never contains what the child wrote. That stays behind your sign-in, for you to read. We keep these records because a concern that nobody can look at is not a safeguard. They are deleted with the account.

Cookies and browser storage

We use one cookie that the service cannot work without:

  • education_session: keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly (unreadable by scripts), SameSite=Strict, sent only over HTTPS, and expires after 8 hours.

We also store small preferences in your browser, which never reach us: your light or dark setting, and a local copy of the current learner view. Clearing your browser data removes them.

We set no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking. If we ever add analytics, it will only run for people who have accepted it, and you can change that choice at any time.

Who else processes it

  • Microsoft Azure: hosting, databases, file storage, the language model and speech
  • Stripe: payments and subscription billing
  • Azure Communication Services: sending the emails we owe you

Each acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

  • While you subscribe, and for a short period afterwards so you can come back
  • Financial records for as long as UK tax law requires
  • Everything else is deleted when you delete the account: profile, progress, safeguarding records and subscription row

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask us for a copy of what we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable form. Write to privacy@y7to11.ai and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you by email before it takes effect rather than quietly updating the date at the top.
