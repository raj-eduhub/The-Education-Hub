Straight answers

Questions parents ask.

Including the awkward ones. If yours isn't here, ask us. A person replies.

Is this just ChatGPT with a school logo on it?

No, and the difference is the whole point. The lessons aren't generated. All 212 topics were written and checked against the published specification before the product opened, and they're served from storage, identically, every time.

There is an AI tutor, and it's genuinely useful for "but why?" questions. It just isn't the thing doing the teaching.

Has it been proven to improve grades?

Not yet, and we won't pretend otherwise. Y7to11.AI is new, so there is no cohort data to point at.

If that matters to you more than anything else, a school-bought product with years of usage behind it is the safer choice. What we offer instead is complete visibility of what's inside, and a subscription you can end after one month.

My child will just get it to do their homework.

It refuses. Requests to simply produce an answer are declined before they reach the model, as are attempts to talk it off the subject, including the creative ones.

What it will do is work through a question with them, one step at a time, and then ask them to finish it.

Which exam board does it follow?

AQA and Edexcel, chosen separately for each subject when you set the account up, because from Year 9 the board changes which questions your child should be practising. Foundation and Higher are set from Year 10 in Maths and Science.

Years 7 and 8 follow the DfE national curriculum programmes of study, which every board builds on.

Is my child safe using it unsupervised?

Every message is screened before it reaches the model. Anything that raises a safeguarding concern is answered by directing your child to a trusted adult, recorded, and emailed to the account's adult straight away.

The alert tells you it happened and which rule was triggered. It never puts what your child wrote in an email. That stays behind your sign-in, for you to read.

Can I see what they've actually been doing?

Yes: topics covered, questions answered, accuracy, and where they're weakest. All of it from marked work rather than anything your child tells it about themselves.

What if my child is behind, or well ahead?

They can work on any topic in their year, in any order, and the tier setting in Maths and Science pitches the work to what they're actually entered for.

The registered school year itself is fixed once set, so it can't be edited to make the work easier.

How do I cancel?

Two clicks in your account, any time, and it runs to the end of the month you've paid for. There's no notice period, no minimum term and no retention phone call.

Still not sure?

That's fair. It's your money and your child's time. Ask us anything before you pay, and a person will answer rather than a form letter.
