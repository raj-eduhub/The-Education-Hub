In plain terms. Y7to11.AI helps your child learn. It is not a school, not an exam board, and not a guarantee of any grade. Use it alongside their teaching, not instead of it.

These terms are the agreement between you and IntelliQuest Solutions Limited when you use Y7to11.AI. By creating an account you accept them.

Who may hold an account

An account must be opened by a parent, guardian or other responsible adult aged 18 or over, for a child in their care. You are responsible for what happens on your account, including keeping your password to yourself.

No affiliation with exam boards or schools

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC, the Department for Education, or any school. Those names are used only to describe which published specification a piece of content was written against.

What we ask about your child

We ask for your child's first name and date of birth, and nothing else. The date of birth is used only to establish which school year they are in, because that decides what they are taught. We do not ask for a surname, a school, an address or any contact details for them.

About you we keep a fuller record (your full name, email address and mobile number), because the account, the contract and the payment are yours rather than your child's, and we may need to reach you about any of them.

This follows the data minimisation standard in the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code. The privacy notice sets out exactly what is held and why.

What we do and do not promise

Our lessons and questions are written against the published DfE programmes of study and the AQA and Edexcel specifications, and checked automatically whenever they change. We work hard to keep them accurate.

Even so:

  • We do not guarantee any grade, result or improvement. Nobody honestly can.
  • Specifications change, and there may be a period before our content catches up.
  • Content may contain errors. If you find one, tell us and we will fix it for everyone.
  • This is a supplement to school teaching, not a replacement for it, and not a substitute for a qualified teacher's judgement about your child.

The AI tutor

The tutor is software. It is built to explain rather than to hand over answers, and it refuses requests to do the work outright, but like any such system it can occasionally be wrong or incomplete. Treat what it says as help with understanding, not as a final authority, and check anything that matters against your child's own materials.

Using it to produce work for submission as a pupil's own, where that breaks a school's or exam board's rules, is not something we support and may end your account.

Acceptable use

  • One subscription covers one child. Do not share account details.
  • Do not attempt to extract, copy or republish our curriculum content.

Our content, software, wording and design remain ours. Your subscription is a licence to use them for your own family, not a transfer of ownership.

Payment and cancellation

The subscription is billed monthly in advance through Stripe, and renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from your account; access continues to the end of the month you have paid for, and we do not refund part-months unless the law requires it.

Introductory pricing applies for the period stated at the time you subscribe. We will give you at least 30 days' notice by email before any change to your rate.

If a payment fails, Stripe will retry over a short period. If it still cannot be taken, the subscription is cancelled and access ends.
